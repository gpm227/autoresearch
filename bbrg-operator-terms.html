Operator terms: Greg and Kirk

Greg and Kirk source, structure, and close the acquisition, then operate the holdco through the succession. Terms below are proposed ranges for the fee letter and holdco agreement, negotiated with Gart. This document never reaches the seller side.

Compensation

1

Diligence work fees

$20K per month, each, from the approved deal budget, payable monthly from letter signature. Expenses at cost. Work fees credit 100% against the closing fee. If a gate fails, fees stop with the budget.

2

Closing fee

2% of total consideration at each closing, paid at funding, less credited work fees. Covers sourcing, structuring, negotiation, and execution. On the slow build it pays per closing as each one funds.

3

Management fee

$400K per year combined plus expenses, from the holdco, reviewed annually against royalty growth. Any portion above the lender coverage covenant accrues rather than lapses, so tight early years defer the fee instead of cutting it. Covers the renewal calendar, franchisee relations alongside Tom, store acquisitions, lender reporting, and the amended FDD program.

4

Distribution participation at the growth threshold

Once trailing twelve month royalties exceed the FY25 base by 15% (about $3.2M) and debt service coverage clears the lender covenant, operators receive 10% of all distributions, before return of Gart's capital. Growth gets proven first, then the operators who built it get paid alongside the capital, not after it.

5

The promote, with an exit ratchet

20% of distributions and exit proceeds above return of Gart's capital plus an 8% preferred, stepping to 30% of everything above a 2.5x multiple on Gart's capital at exit or sale. Split between Greg and Kirk per their separate agreement. Structured as a profits interest at grant, targeting capital gain treatment. Vests over the succession period, accelerates on a sale of the platform. Co invest rights alongside Gart at cost.

Roles

G

Greg · managing operator

Leads the transaction and the holdco. Owns the economic grid, the seller negotiations, the lender relationship, the renewal calendar, and reporting to Gart. Greg holds one permanent seat on the holdco board, held for as long as he holds equity or the promote, not subject to removal or vesting. Gart controls the balance of the board plus the consent list.

K

Kirk · operating partner

Owns the bottom up store audit, the per store schedule, store level economics, and operations post close: franchisee support, the expansion pipeline, and real estate execution.

Guardrails

·

Subordination

The promote sits behind LC repayment and Gart's preferred. Distribution participation switches on only at the growth threshold with coverage cleared. No fees on Gart's capital draws. Management fee above the coverage covenant accrues.

·

Gart consent rights

Acquisitions above a set threshold, new debt, affiliate transactions, changes to the operator terms, and any sale of the platform. Everything touching the deal team prices at arm's length and gets documented.

·

Tax

Work and management fees are ordinary income. The promote targets capital gain as a profits interest, subject to the holding period rules. Counsel papers the grants before closing.
