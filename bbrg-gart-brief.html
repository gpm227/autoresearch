What we refuse · the purgatory states

Dead end 1

Marks without the machine

JJ&N's brand in hand but no BBRG: a logo with zero royalty contracts. Nothing to collect. Never buy the marks alone.

Dead end 2

Machine without the marks

BBRG without the license secured: $2.76M of royalties that die store-by-store at each FA expiry — Item 13 says franchisees keep the marks only for their existing terms. A melting annuity.

Dead end 3

Control without an endgame

55% plus a hostile 45% and no contractual sweep: deadlock, and every dispute lands in our own Item 3, in front of every prospect. Control must come with the mechanics that finish the job.

The closing — everything funds together, or nothing funds

One cross-conditioned signing: Tom's 55%, the marks (sale or re-papered license), and — per the OA — the minority same-day or bound into the sweep. The LC is never exposed to a partial state. If the 45% follows later, the sweep runs on contract, not hope: blended offer under NDA / no-shop / short fuse, their two stores pinned by the 15-day match right on any bona fide offer, consent required for any equity transfer in a store entity (the "99% sale" can't happen quietly — unapproved transfers are void), and the renewal calendar stepping every legacy store to 5% + 1% on its contractual date.

Then the machine runs · where money comes from, in order

1

Weekly cash from day one

5% of gross revenues + 1% Creative Fund (raisable to 2% on 90 days' notice), collected every Wednesday — 52 paydays a year. Items 5–6

2

Contractual raises on a published calendar

Renewal requires the then-current form, which "WILL CONTAIN THE ROYALTY… PAYABLE BY NEW… FRANCHISEES." Legacy 4% stores step up at renewal or at expiry — their own FA sets the date, never the amount. No rate moves mid-term; none survives the paper's end. FA §5.2(d) Item 17(c)

3

Turnover upgrades the paper

Every consented store sale can require the buyer to sign the current form, plus a $10,000 transfer fee. FA §12.2(c)(iv), (b)(vii)

4

Store buys at market-proven prices

Any real offer must be shown to us in full, and we can take the same deal within 15 days — acquisitions at prices a third party already validated. FA §12.3(b)–(c)

5

The earnout pays itself

Collected from the royalty stream the seller's own audits prove — the Garts fund the provable part; performance funds the rest. Item 21 / Exhibit B
